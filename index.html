Dripotastik · Android app

Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

The short version. Dripotastik has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your clothes, photos, outfit history, comfort answers and calendar stay on your phone.

The only thing that leaves your phone is your town's rough location (or the city you search for), which is sent to the free weather service Open-Meteo to get the forecast.

Who we are

Dripotastik ("the app") is a free Android app made by an independent developer (donevdarko on GitHub). It suggests what to wear from the weather, your calendar and the clothes you add. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

What the app uses, and where it goes

DataWhat it's forLeaves your phone?
Approximate location (optional) Finding your town for the weather. The app only asks for approximate (city-level) location, never precise location, and only when you tap "Use my location". You can type a city instead. Yes: the coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, are sent to Open-Meteo over an encrypted connection to get the forecast. This also happens in the background for the morning notification and the home-screen widget, using the place you saved.
City search Finding a town by name if you don't share your location. Yes: the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's place search.
Calendar (optional, read only) Today's event titles and times, to work out when you're out and spot occasions like an interview or the gym. No. Events are read on your phone and are not stored or sent anywhere.
Photos Pictures of your clothes, care labels and outfits, taken with your camera app or picked with Android's photo picker. No. They are kept in the app's private storage. The app can't see your other photos.
Wardrobe, outfit history, comfort answers, settings Recommending outfits, tracking laundry and learning whether you run warm or cold. No. Stored only on your phone.
Notifications The optional morning outfit notification. No. Built on your phone.

Open-Meteo

Weather forecasts and place search come from Open-Meteo.com. Like any website you contact, Open-Meteo receives your device's IP address with each request. Its handling of that data is described in its terms and privacy information. We don't send Open-Meteo anything that identifies you, and we receive nothing back about you.

What we don't do

  • We don't run servers that receive your data, so we never see your wardrobe, photos, location or calendar.
  • We don't sell or share personal data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.
  • We don't use advertising IDs or track you across apps or websites.

Backups

In Settings you can save a backup file of your wardrobe, photos, history and settings to a place you choose (for example your Google Drive) using Android's file picker. That file goes only where you send it; we don't receive it. Android may also include the app's data in your phone's own Google backup, depending on your phone's backup settings. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete single items or outfits in the app, or everything at once by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

Security

Weather requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Everything else is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

Children

Dripotastik is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact
